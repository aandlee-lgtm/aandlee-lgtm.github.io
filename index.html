Sydney Fare Watch

A personal watch for good-value return flights from Sydney (SYD), planned 3–12 months ahead. All prices in Australian dollars (A$).

Destinations watched

EuropeJapanChina
  • Europe: Paris and London first; Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Munich, Rome, Milan, Madrid, Barcelona and Zurich when the price is exceptional. No connections through the Middle East or Türkiye.
  • Japan: Tokyo and Osaka (for Kyoto), plus Fukuoka, Nagoya, Sapporo, Okinawa and Hiroshima, including open-jaw trips.
  • China: Guangzhou and Shanghai, plus Beijing, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Chongqing, Hangzhou and Xiamen.

How it works

  • Fares are checked daily for return trips of about 1–6 weeks, economy first.
  • China Southern and Virgin Australia are checked every day.
  • Every fare comes with a link to a live search, where the current price is confirmed before booking.
  • Prices found by searches are indicative: they can change or sell out, and are never shown as guaranteed.
